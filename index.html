I recently completed my Ph.D. at the School of Computer Science, Fudan University, under the supervision of Yunlei Zhao. I will soon join the Max Planck Institute for Security and Privacy (MPI-SP) as a postdoctoral researcher and am currently waiting for my visa. My research focuses on post-quantum cryptography, cryptographic engineering, post-quantum TLS, and high-assurance / machine-checked cryptography.
